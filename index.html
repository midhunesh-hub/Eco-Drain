Why EcoDrain

Plastic Waste → Blocked Drain → Waterlogging

EcoDrain intercepts waste at the source and continuously monitors drainage conditions using IoT, so cleaning happens before a blockage — not after.

🌧️ Rainfall → 🗑️ Waste Trapped → 📡 Sensor Reads Level → 🔔 Alert Raised → 🧹 Drain Cleaned
How It Works

Four Simple Stages

01

Catch

A removable mesh trap intercepts plastic and leaves before they enter the drainage channel.

02

Sense

An ultrasonic sensor on the ESP32 measures the trap's fill level in real time.

03

Alert

Flask automatically classifies the reading and raises an alert when cleaning is required.

04

Act

Maintenance staff clean the trap and log it — the dashboard returns to normal instantly.
